02 / EXPERIENCE

Research & projects

Academic research experience spanning experimental molecular biology, structural biology, biomechanics, computational biophysics, and biomedical data science.

2026 · JUL–AUG Bioinformatics

Ontology Mapping and Data Processing of the ITHANET Database

Cyprus Institute of Neurology & Genetics (CING)

Research internship at the Blood Disorder Genetics and Thalassaemia Department, focusing on the standardisation, interoperability, and processing of biomedical and genetic variant data.

The first part of the project involved mapping terminology from the ITHANET databases to established biomedical ontologies, contributing to the FAIRification of the data. Ontologies included SNOMED CT, NCIT, MONDO, and the Human Phenotype Ontology (HPO).

The second part focused on improving an existing R Markdown pipeline for processing IthaGenes genetic variant data for submission to ClinVar. Modifications included retaining potentially relevant variants, preserving dated export results, and comparing successive datasets to identify changes requiring verification.

Methods and tools: R, Python, R Markdown, Ontotext Refine, ontology mapping, biomedical data standardisation, and genetic variant validation.

Supervised by Dr. Petros Kountouris, Dr. Coralia Stephanou and Maria Xenophontos.

2025 · SEP–DEC Biomechanics

Quantitative Assessment of Collagen Hydrogel Stiffness

Laboratory of Solid Mechanics (LMS) · École Polytechnique

Experimental project investigating the mechanical properties of collagen hydrogels prepared under different polymerisation conditions.

Collagen gels were prepared at different concentrations to investigate the influence of gel composition and preparation conditions on stiffness.

The project introduced experimental approaches to soft biomaterials, mechanical characterisation, and the relationship between material structure and mechanical behaviour.

Research areas: Collagen hydrogels, soft biomaterials, experimental mechanics, and stiffness measurements.

Supervisor: Prof. Jean-Marc Allain.

2025 · JUN–JUL Structural biology

Cryo-Electron Microscopy and Ribosomal Complexes

BIOC · École Polytechnique

Research internship involving structural biology approaches to investigate ribosomal complexes and proteins involved in translation regulation.

Experimental work included protein production and purification, in vitro reconstitution of ribosomal complexes, and preparation of samples for cryo-electron microscopy.

The project also provided experience with cryo-EM data collection and processing, molecular visualisation, and interpretation of structural data.

Methods and tools: Protein purification, ribosomal complex reconstitution, cryo-EM sample preparation, CryoSPARC, ChimeraX, and Coot.

Supervised by Prof. Clément Madru.

2024 · JUL–AUG Computational biophysics

Protein Design and Molecular Modelling

University of Cyprus

Computational biophysics research project focused on modifying protein interactions through computational protein design.

The objective was to investigate potential improvements in the binding affinity between AnkB and GABARAP by exploring amino acid modifications using Monte Carlo methods implemented in the Proteus software.

Molecular structures and interactions were visualised using Visual Molecular Dynamics (VMD).

Methods and tools: Computational protein design, Monte Carlo methods, Proteus, molecular modelling, and VMD.

Supervised by Prof. Georgios Archontis and Prof. Savvas Polydoridis.

2025–2026 Bachelor thesis

Study of the Function of the uS2 Protein in Translation Initiation

BIOC · École Polytechnique

Bachelor research thesis investigating the role of the uS2 ribosomal protein in the stability of translation initiation complexes.

The project aimed to investigate the interaction between uS2 and the 30S ribosomal subunit, particularly through the determination of a dissociation constant.

Experimental work involved site-directed mutagenesis, DNA amplification and purification, protein expression and purification, and fluorescence anisotropy measurements.

The study explored the effects of selected protein mutations on ribosomal interactions, providing experience in experimental design, troubleshooting, and quantitative biochemical analysis.

Methods and tools: Site-directed mutagenesis, PCR, DNA purification, protein purification, and fluorescence anisotropy.

Supervised by Prof. Emmanuelle Schmitt, Prof. Clément Madru and PhD student Valentina Varriale.

2025 · SEP–NOV Mathematics & communication

Mathematical Crochet Project

École Polytechnique

Interdisciplinary project exploring the physical representation of mathematical objects through textile techniques.

Created crocheted mathematical surfaces and geometric structures, including work on the representation of Boy's surface.

The creations were presented during a Bachelor event, demonstrating how hands-on visualisation can communicate mathematical concepts.
